Biography

Prophet Muhammad s.a.w. was born in Mecca in the year 570 CE. Orphaned at a young age, he was raised by his grandfather and later his uncle "Abu Talib".

He was known as "Al Amin" (The trustworthy), he worked as a merchant and was respected for his honesty and integrity.

At the age of 40, he received the first revelation from Allah(GOD) through "Malaku Jibreel" (Angel Gabriel) in the cave of hira

Over the next 23 years, he delivered the message of Islam, calling people to worship one Allah and to uphold justice, compassion and mercy.

Key Life Events

  • 570 CE: Birth in Mecca
  • 595 CE: Marriage to Khadija (R.A)
  • 610 CE: First revelation in the Cave of Hira
  • 622 CE: Hijra(migration) to Madinah
  • 630 CE: Peaceful conquest of Mecca
  • 632 CE: Farewell Sermon and passing away in Madinah
